🎓 One Thing to Learn

Evolution strategies (ES) are a type of optimization algorithm that can be used to improve the performance of AI systems. They work by sampling a population of perturbed copies of the model, scoring each one, and moving in the reward-weighted direction of the perturbations. Understanding how ES works and how to apply it to AI models can help builders improve the efficiency and effectiveness of their systems.
