This has significant implications for the future of video content creation and the potential for AI-generated videos to become indistinguishable from human-created ones. [Paper PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09894v1.pdf)
- **[On Agentic Manipulation Policies](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09892v1)** by Chen Chen, Omid Bagherieh, Naveen Nikamanth Appiah Balaji, Jesse T. Gonzalez et al. — shows that generalist AI models can control manipulators more reliably than specialist policies such as vision-language-action models and world-action models, and provides insights into the challenges of developing effective agentic manipulation policies. This research has significant implications for the development of AI-powered robotics and the potential for AI to revolutionize industries such as manufacturing and logistics. [Paper PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09892v1.pdf)
- **[CPU-ARC: Agentic Reasoning Caching for on-CPU Reasoning Reuse](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09891v1)** by Peter Belcak, Greg Heinrich, Saurav Muralidharan, Shizhe Diao et al. — introduces agentic reasoning caching, a method for constructing robust CPU-executable agentic reasoning caches from LLM reasoning traces, and demonstrates its effectiveness in achieving on-par performance with frontier LLMs at a significantly lower cost.
