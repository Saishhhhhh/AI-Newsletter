## 📄 Papers Worth Reading
- **[SceneBloom: Agentic Indoor Environment Generation with Realistic and Diverse Layouts for Embodied Learning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32978v1)** by Yuhan Liu, Lianhui Qin, Shenji Wan — SceneBloom's agentic framework generates realistic indoor environments by combining visual layout sampling with contract-guided 3D compilation. This approach enables the creation of physically valid and simulation-ready scenes, which is crucial for training generalizable agents. [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32978v1.pdf)
- **[RobustRLlib: A Unified Library and Benchmark for Robust Reinforcement Learning Algorithms](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32971v1)** by Xi Chen, Fengqi Hu, Zeyu Shen, Jiaying Xu, Shangding Gu, Pan Xu, Anqi Liu, Laixi Shi — RobustRLlib integrates 17 robust RL algorithms, providing a comprehensive platform for evaluating and developing robust RL methods. Its systematic analysis across diverse real-world shifts can help AI builders identify the most effective algorithms for their specific use cases. [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32971v1.pdf)
- **[DexPort: A Hand-Held Skill Interface for Dexterous Hand Data Collection](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32969v1)** by Amber Xie, Jiawei Fu, Xiaofeng Guo, Carmelo Sferrazza, Guanya Shi, Haozhi Qi — DexPort simplifies demonstration collection for dexterous manipulation tasks by using the same hand hardware for collection and deployment. This method reduces the hand embodiment gap, making it easier to train AI models for complex actions. [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.32969v1.pdf)
