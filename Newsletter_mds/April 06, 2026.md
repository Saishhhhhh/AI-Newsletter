## 📄 Papers Worth Reading  
- **[Enhancing Robustness of Federated Learning via Server Learning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03226v1)** by Van Sy Mai et al.  
  Proposes a server‑side learner that filters malicious client updates using geometric‑median aggregation.  
  Gives you a plug‑and‑play robustness layer for cross‑device model training, reducing the need for heavy client‑side defenses.  

- **[Coupled Control, Structured Memory, and Verifiable Action in Agentic AI (SCRAT)](http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03201v1)** by Maximiliano Armesto & Christophe Kolb.  
  Introduces a hierarchical controller with latent dynamics, episodic memory, and built‑in verification signals.  
  Blueprint for building agents that can self‑audit, handle delayed feedback, and resist hidden‑state drift.  

- **[PR3DICTR: A modular AI framework for medical 3D image‑based detection and outcome prediction](http://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03203v1)** by Daniel C. MacRae et al.  
  Delivers a PyTorch/MONAI‑based plug‑in system for 3‑D classification with two‑line model definition.  
  Shows how a standardized module hub can cut prototype time from weeks to days for any volumetric AI task.

## 🎓 One Thing to Learn  
**Server‑Side Learning for Federated Robustness** – Instead of trusting every client to filter its own data, the central server runs a lightweight learner that predicts the “true” update direction from noisy, potentially adversarial client gradients. It then applies a geometric‑median filter before aggregation. This approach cuts the malicious client tolerance threshold from ~30 % to >50 % while requiring only a synthetic seed dataset on the server. For builders, it means you can launch FL services with far fewer security audits and still guarantee model integrity.
